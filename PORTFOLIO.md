## What This Project Says About Me as an AI Engineer

I'm someone who prioritizes **honesty over perfection**. When I discovered the system was scoring recommendations against fake, simulated audio features, I didn't try to engineer my way out of the problem with smarter algorithms. Instead, I stepped back and asked: "Why are we using fake data?" The solution was switching to a real Spotify dataset—simple, honest, and far more effective. This reflects my core belief that great AI systems start with great data and transparency about limitations.

I'm also a **critical collaborator with AI tools**. Throughout this project, I worked extensively with Claude but refused to blindly accept every suggestion. When Claude proposed building a machine learning model with synthetic labels for mood inference, I recognized it was solving the wrong problem. The real breakthrough came from looking at working code (Bug Hound project) rather than abstract troubleshooting. This taught me that the best AI collaboration happens when you combine AI's creativity with your domain knowledge and healthy skepticism.

I'm **product-focused and user-aware**. The mood caching system I built isn't just technically sound—it solves a real, tangible problem (finding songs that genuinely match the mood you want) in a way users can understand and trust. I designed it to be honest about what it can do (accurate mood matching on 603 curated songs) rather than pretending to do everything (real-time search for any song). That tradeoff reflects how I approach engineering: solve the right problem well, not all problems poorly.

Finally, I am **music lover**. I enjoy the thrill when a song recommended by an app that fits perfectly my mood, my vibe while expanding my listening horizon. Building this app is very tricky but I was highly committed to cruise through the challenges and build a workable product.
